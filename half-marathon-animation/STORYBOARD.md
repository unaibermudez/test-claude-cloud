# Storyboard: "Clawd runs a half marathon"

**Logline:** Clawd wants to run a half marathon (21.1 km) in six weeks. It follows the plan (a steady weekly rhythm, a long run that grows, easy efforts, good food and good sleep), so on race day it starts slow and finishes strong.

**What it explains.** This is the animated version of the "Half Marathon 6-Week Plan" slide deck:
- the training week (rest, easy, strength, quality, rest, short easy, long run)
- the six-week progression of the long run (10 → 12 → 14 → 16 → 12 → 21.1 km) and its three phases (base, strength, taper)
- pacing by effort (easy most of the time, about 80%)
- nutrition (carbs, protein, veg, water, and a gel on the long runs)
- recovery (7–9 h of sleep, stretching)
- race-day pacing (start slow, hold steady, finish strong)

**World:** a picture-book countryside on warm paper. Every training type has a colour, used everywhere:
rest = sky blue, easy = sap green, strength = violet, quality = clay, long run = ochre/gold.

**Colour arc:** cool morning greens → the cool blue of night → golden race-day dawn.

**Motif:** the finish arch with its "21.1 km" banner. It's far away in the first shot, and Clawd breaks its tape in the last.

**Clawd's arc:** unsure → determined → working hard → rested → confident → proud.

**Text.** The kit's guide says no text. Because this video has to *explain* a plan, it uses a few painted words and numbers: day initials, week numbers, distances, "80%", "7–9 h" and the banner. There are no captions or sentences.
